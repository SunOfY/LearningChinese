v5.3.2 hotfix

Fixed:
- In repeat-practice mode, the 4 writing cells after the model could draw ink at the wrong position.

Root cause:
- The canvas for each practice cell was first sized while the row was still expanding.
- Later, CSS auto-fit changed the cell width, but the canvas internal coordinate system kept the old size.
- Result: Apple Pencil/touch input no longer matched the visible cell position.

Fix:
- After all 8 cells are in the DOM and the final layout is stable, the app now resizes every copybook writing canvas again before use.

v5.3.3 hotfix

Fixed:
- Pressing “Xóa hàng / Clear row” could leave pieces of handwriting behind or make old strokes reappear.

Root causes:
1. clearRect() was clearing in CSS coordinates while the canvas backing bitmap uses device-pixel dimensions.
2. resizeWritingCanvas() restored old ink asynchronously through Image.onload. If that callback completed after Clear, old handwriting could be drawn back.

Fix:
- Clear the entire backing bitmap with an identity transform.
- Preserve ink during resize synchronously with an offscreen canvas, eliminating the delayed redraw race.
- Reset active Pencil state and drawing styles after clearing.

v4.8.5 - Integrated stroke-order preview

What changed
- Removed the large separate stroke-order section below Writing practice.
- Every writing square now contains its own small ▶ button.
- Tap ▶ to animate the stroke order directly inside the same square you will write in.
- During animation, drawing is temporarily disabled to prevent accidental Apple Pencil marks.
- After animation finishes, the faint guide returns if "Show faint guide" is enabled.
- A small stroke-count badge appears in the top-left of each square.
- Apple Pencil anti-selection protections remain enabled.

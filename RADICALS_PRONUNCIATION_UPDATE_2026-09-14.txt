Important learning note:
Character explanations are learning-oriented structural analyses. Many Chinese characters are phonosemantic (semantic clue + sound clue), but not every character literally developed by simply adding a modern radical. Historical sound changes mean some phonetic components no longer match modern Mandarin perfectly.

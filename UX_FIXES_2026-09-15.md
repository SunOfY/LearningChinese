# UX fixes — 2026-09-15

- The website now opens the last section the learner was using; first-time visitors default to **Bộ thủ**.
- The selected main tab is synced in the existing account progress payload, so signed-in learners resume the same section on another device.
- The selected sub-mode inside Bộ thủ (Từ hình đến chữ / Bộ gợi nghĩa / Họ âm / Pinyin) is also remembered and cloud-synced.
- Character Origins now attempts a historical-form chain for every character: oracle-bone → bronze → big-seal → small-seal. This applies to combined and phono-semantic characters too, not only pictograms.
- Combined / phono-semantic screens now show the ancient form between the component analysis and the modern character.
- Compact AI pronunciation results no longer open a floating panel over the content. They show a small inline score/status beside the microphone button.

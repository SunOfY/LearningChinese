v4.8.6 iPad preview fix

Issue:
- On iPad/iPhone Safari, tapping the ▶ stroke-order button could do nothing.
- The writing square intentionally prevented touch gestures to stop text selection; that ancestor preventDefault also suppressed the button tap on Safari.

Fix:
- Preview-button touches are excluded from the canvas gesture blocker.
- The button uses pointerup/touchend/click with de-duplication for Safari compatibility.
- touch-action: manipulation is applied to the preview button.
